## 📌 Quick Reference: Brand Core & Elevator Pitch

- **The Problem:** Freelancers & agencies spend hours drafting proposals, guess their project quotes, undercharge by 30-50%, and juggle 5 different tools (Notion, Google Docs, Excel, Invoice apps, Email).
- **The Solution:** **Floorly** — an intelligent, all-in-one studio operating system that turns messy client briefs into scoped proposals, calculates exact pricing floors, tracks milestone deliverables, and collects payments via Stripe & Razorpay.
- **Key Features:**
  1. 🧠 **AI Project Scoper & Pricing Engine:** Uncovers hidden scope creep, calculates risk multipliers, and gives 3 pricing tiers (Minimum, Target, Value-Based).
  2. 📄 **Interactive Branded Proposals:** Modern, client-facing proposals with instant digital signature approvals.
  3. 💳 **Global & Domestic Invoicing:** Multi-currency support (USD + INR), automated milestones, Razorpay & Stripe checkouts.
  4. 📊 **Financial Dashboard & Cash Flow:** Tracks profit margins, monthly revenue, pending receivables, and expense breakdowns.
  5. ⚖️ **Contract & Clause Library:** Bulletproof contract templates with IP transfer clauses and revision caps.
