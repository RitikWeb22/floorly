## 💬 2. Maker's First Comment (Pinned Launch Comment)

*Post this immediately when your product goes live to kick off the community discussion:*

```markdown
Hey Product Hunt! 👋 I'm the maker behind Floorly, and I'm beyond excited to share it with you all today. 🚀

### 💡 What inspired this?
If you've ever freelanced or run an independent agency, you know this painful cycle:
1. A client sends a vague brief: "We just need a simple redesign."
2. You pick a fixed price out of thin air just to win the client quickly.
3. Two weeks in, the project balloons with 5 unexpected integrations and endless revisions.
4. You end up earning less than minimum wage on what looked like a great deal.

Most freelancers don't struggle because of poor craft—they struggle because of **scope creep and arbitrary pricing**. 

Existing tools (Notion, Google Docs, standard invoicing apps) are passive: they let you send whatever number you type, even when you are severely undercharging yourself.

### 🛡️ How Floorly fixes this:
I built Floorly to act as an intelligent business partner that protects your bottom line:

1. **🧠 AI Scope & Risk Analyzer:** Paste any client email or brief. Floorly extracts the actual technical deliverables, uncovers hidden edge cases, and calculates a Minimum Floor, Target Price, and Value-Based quote.
2. **🛑 The Price Floor Guardrail:** You define your break-even baseline. Floorly refuses to let you quote below that safety floor, no matter the temptation.
3. **📄 Interactive Client Proposals:** High-converting, modern web proposals that clients can review and digitally sign in seconds.
4. **💳 Milestone Invoicing & Payments:** Built-in multi-currency invoicing with instant Razorpay (UPI/INR) and Stripe (USD) checkouts.
5. **📊 Real Profit Ledger:** Real-time visibility into income, expenses, and cash flow so you always know where your business stands.

### 🎁 We'd love your feedback!
Floorly is completely live today at **https://floorlystudio.me**

- How do you currently calculate project quotes?
- Have you ever regretted accepting a fixed-price project?

I'll be hanging out here all day to answer your questions, listen to your feedback, and take feature requests. Thank you so much for the support! ❤️
```
